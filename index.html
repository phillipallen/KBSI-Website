Phillip Allen

Founder & Principal Systems Engineer

Phillip founded Kush BSI to bring systems engineering discipline to the decisions that come before code: what to build, how to buy it, and how to prove it works. He spent 15 years in federal civil service and now supports Navy personnel systems as a contractor, working in project management, requirements management, human-centered design, business process reengineering and Agile delivery.

He has designed problem-solving and process reengineering frameworks for Navy personnel organizations, helping teams define problems clearly, align stakeholders and turn needs into requirements that contractors can build against.

Phillip is based in Chesapeake, Virginia, close to the Navy community in Hampton Roads and within reach of the National Capital Region.
